Featured AI Research

Med-GReF Research Paper

Evidence-Guided Multimodal Fusion and Hallucination Verification for Medical Vision-Language Reasoning

Submitted to NeurIPS 2026 (40th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)

Med-GReF: Medical Grounded Reasoning & Fusion

13,216 Vetted PMC Figures 4-Pass Code Audit
Abstract & Objective

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) applied to medical imaging often produce claims that sound fluent but aren't backed by the visual or clinical evidence in front of them — a failure mode with direct patient-safety consequences.

Med-GReF pairs frozen vision-language backbones (BiomedCLIP and PubMedBERT) with quantitative radiomics, Grad-CAM visual explanations, an evidence-guided cross-attention fusion network, and a dedicated Natural Language Inference (NLI) verifier.

Key Impact Metrics
Classification Accuracy: 0.817 → 0.912 (±0.020)
ROC-AUC Improvement: 0.570 → 0.881 (±0.016)
Hallucination Reduction: 2.4× Cut (16.6% → 7.0%)
Hallucination Catch Rate: 65.5% vs 10.3% baseline
Architectural Innovations & Methodology
Multimodal Fusion Stream

Combines 4 streams: BiomedCLIP vision, PubMedBERT clinical text, 64D GLCM radiomics, and Grad-CAM visual saliency with dynamic modality gating.

NLI Hallucination Verifier

Dedicated entailment module scoring whether stated claims match retrieved visual evidence, yielding calibrated confidence scores.

MedGemma-4B QLoRA

Fine-tuned 4-bit QLoRA generative MedGemma-4B backbone on RunPod RTX 4090 GPU hardware across 3-seed evaluation splits.

Methodological Integrity 4 Audit Passes

Conducted 4 rigorous audit passes over evaluation and fine-tuning pipelines. Identified and resolved critical subtle bugs including untrained confidence heads, unseeded randomness, single-class evaluation splits, and cross-attention tensor shape errors before publication reporting.
